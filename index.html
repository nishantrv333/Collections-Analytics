Collections Analytics

An AI collections agent working for four banks: Northgate (UK), Desert Finance (UAE), Banco Meridia (Spain) and Lumen Pay (Mexico), June to September 2026. The platform data is simulated; exchange rates, public holidays and calling rules are real, and customer behaviour is calibrated on real bank data.

Plans agreed

Every promise is checked against the money that actually arrives. An instalment counts as kept when at least 95% of what was due lands within 3 days of the due date; a payment that is later reversed does not count.

Weekend payments missing from euro totals

Share of each bank's money that the first version dropped, because ECB rates are only published on weekdays. Fixed by carrying the last rate forward over weekends and holidays.

The platform data (accounts, calls, promises, payments) is simulated, because call level collections data tied to payments is never published. Exchange rates are the ECB's daily reference rates, public holidays are the real 2026 calendars, and calling hours follow each market's rules. How customers behave is calibrated on two real public datasets: UCI Bank Marketing (41,188 bank calls; Moro, Cortez and Rita, 2014) and UCI Default of Credit Card Clients (30,000 card customers; Yeh and Lien, 2009), both CC BY 4.0. Every number here is produced by python run_all.py in the repository.
